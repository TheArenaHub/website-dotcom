About Arena Hestia

Built the way a good parent works through a hard day

Not a mission statement written for a pitch deck — the actual reasoning behind the tool.

Arena's founder learned how to solve a problem from his own father, long before he ever built anything.

His father was a calm, quiet man. If you came to him with a problem, he didn't lecture you or panic — he sat with you, worked through it together, and helped you take the next step. That's the whole idea behind Hestia: not a system that judges how the day went, but a quiet place to plan it, and somewhere to note what actually happened, without ever having to reconstruct it from memory at nine o'clock at night.

Seen for what you're actually managing

Years later, Arena's founder spent time working directly in classrooms. One moment stayed with him: a student, quietly dismissed by a system that had no way to see what he was actually capable of — labelled as lacking focus, rather than recognised as poorly served by how the school recorded and understood him.

Parents educating a child at home hit the same wall from the other side. You know exactly what your child managed today — the good morning, the meltdown that made sense once you knew the trigger, the thing they finally got the hang of. But if a Local Authority, a school, or your own memory a month from now is the only place that's recorded, it's gone the moment you stop holding it in your head.

Hestia isn't here to tell you how to parent, or to replace your own judgement about your child. It exists so the things you already notice — the plan for the day, what actually happened, the pattern only you can see over weeks — have somewhere to live that isn't just your memory.

Your record, kept the way it should be

Everything Hestia captures lives in your own Google account — not a shared database, not something sold or profiled, not visible to your child's school or anyone else unless you actively choose to share it. That's the same principle behind every Arena product, applied here to something as personal as your own family's day-to-day: your evidence belongs to you, not to a system that files it away.

Arena also publishes longer-form research and governance thinking — safeguarding, AI, SEND, and educational policy — through The Arena Hub Paradigm, kept deliberately separate from anything commercial.
