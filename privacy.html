Privacy Policy

How The Arena Hub Ltd collects, uses and protects information in Arena Lite and Arena Hestia — including data received from Google APIs.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who We Are

The Arena Hub Ltd ("Arena Hub", "we", "us") is a company registered in England and Wales, Company No. 17086105. This policy covers both of our apps, which sign in with Google under the name "Arena Hub":

Arena Lite — an evidence capture and planning platform for Alternative Provision and SEMH education practitioners.
Arena Hestia — a daily planning and evidence platform for parents and carers of children learning at home.

Contact for all data-related matters: data-protection@thearenahub.co.uk.

2. Data Controller and Data Processor

Data Controller: the subscribing practitioner, school or organisation (Arena Lite), or the parent/carer household (Arena Hestia). You decide what is recorded about the learners in your care.

Data Processor: The Arena Hub Ltd. We process data only on your behalf and on your instructions, as set out in our Data Processing Agreement.

Learner records — observations, evidence, plans, routines and safeguarding entries — are stored in your own Google Drive, not on Arena Hub's systems. When you use a feature, the request passes through our server (Google Cloud Run, London) to carry out that action and is not stored by us afterwards (see sections 5 and 6).

3. What Data We Hold About You

Account data: your name, email address and setting, organisation or household name, provided when you sign up.

Licence data: your licence key, plan, start and expiry dates and status.

Payment data: handled entirely by Stripe, Inc. We never see or store card numbers.

Account and licence data is held in our Command Centre records and Google Cloud Firestore in the London (europe-west2) region.

4. Google User Data: What We Access and Why

When you sign in, Google asks you to allow "Arena Hub" the following permissions. Each one is used only for the purpose shown:

See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files that you use with this app (drive.file) — to create your Evidence Vault folders in your Drive and to save evidence files you choose to upload (including through the Google file picker). The apps can only see files they created or that you selected; they cannot see the rest of your Drive.

See, edit, create and delete your Google Sheets spreadsheets (spreadsheets) — to read and write your own Evidence Vault spreadsheet, which the app creates in your Drive at setup and which holds your learner records and evidence log.

See, edit, create and delete your Google Docs documents (documents) — to create reports, plans and evidence summaries as Google Docs in your Drive (some are then saved as PDF in your Exports folder).

Connect to an external service (script.external_request) — to contact Arena Hub's own server (Google Cloud Run, London) to check your licence and to provide AI planning and evidence suggestions (section 5).

Your Google account email address (userinfo.email) — to match the signed-in Google account to the licence issued to that email address.

We do not request access to Gmail, your contacts, your calendar or the rest of your Google Drive.

5. AI Features

Some features — such as Plan the Day, HIVEMIND session plans, curriculum matching and Arena Lite's document descriptions — use Google's Gemini models through Google Cloud Vertex AI, processed in the London (europe-west2) region.

What is sent: only the information needed for the feature you are using, for example the text you have written (an evidence description, notes or a session topic), the learner's age, key stage or working level, and, in Arena Lite, a document you attach as evidence (it is automatically sent to the AI to draft a description, which you then review and edit before saving). Arena Hub does not attach learners' names, dates of birth or contact details to AI requests. Because text and attached documents are sent as you provide them, please avoid including names or other identifying details in them.

What is not done: AI requests and responses are not stored in Arena Hub's databases, and Google Cloud does not use this data to train its AI models. Arena Hub does not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models.

6. Who Else Processes Data (Sub-processors)

Google Cloud (Cloud Run, Firestore, Vertex AI — London, europe-west2): hosting, licence records and AI processing.

Google Maps Platform (Arena Hestia local-learning ideas only): the area or postcode you enter and the learning topic, to suggest nearby venues. No learner details are sent.

Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, EU): sends service emails such as your welcome and activation email (your name, email address and licence key).

Stripe, Inc.: payment processing.

We do not sell data, use advertising networks or use analytics trackers.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

Arena Hub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

• Google user data is used only to provide and improve the features you use in Arena Lite or Arena Hestia.
• It is not transferred to others except as needed to provide those features (section 6), to comply with law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with your notice.
• It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never used to determine creditworthiness.
• No person at Arena Hub reads it unless you ask us to (for example for support), it is needed for security purposes such as investigating abuse, or the law requires it.
• It is not used to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models.

8. Removing Access and Deleting Data

To remove Arena Hub's access to your Google account: go to your Google Account → Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services (myaccount.google.com/connections), choose "Arena Hub" and remove access.

Learner data is in your own Google Drive; you can delete the Arena folders and spreadsheet at any time. Account and licence data is kept for your subscription and up to 30 days after it ends, then deleted. You can ask us to delete it sooner by emailing data-protection@thearenahub.co.uk.

9. Legal Basis

Contract (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) for account, licence and service delivery; legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) for security, fraud prevention and essential service messages. Marketing emails are only sent with your opt-in consent.

10. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

You can ask for access to, correction of, erasure of, or a portable copy of your personal data, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact data-protection@thearenahub.co.uk; we respond within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

11. Cookies

The apps use only the essential session cookies Google sets to keep you signed in. This website uses no analytics, tracking or advertising cookies.

12. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes we will email active subscribers before they take effect. The date at the top of this page shows the latest revision.
